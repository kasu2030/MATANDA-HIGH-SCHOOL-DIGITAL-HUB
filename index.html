Education 5.0 in action

One home for Matanda's teaching, research and local innovation.

A single place where learners, teachers and the Matanda community share notes, research, notices and the pathways connecting our school to local industry — built so nothing valuable gets lost between a classroom and a village meeting.

Browse E-Learning Resources Read Community Notices

The five pillars this hub is built around

  1. Teaching — subject notes, past papers, tutorials
  2. Research — student research from our own community
  3. Community Service — notices and a direct feedback line
  4. Innovation — a gallery of learner-built projects
  5. Industrialisation — local industry and career pathways

Why this hub exists: consultation with students, staff and community members showed that Matanda High School and the surrounding community had no centralised platform for sharing information — so school programmes, career guidance and local innovation news often didn't reach the students and parents who needed them.

Hosting decision

Free hosting was chosen over a paid domain, since this is a school WIL project. GitHub Pages was selected: no cost, no renewal, and a stable link that can be shared with the school and community.
